# Readable fallbacks

These examples show the current terminal limitations explicitly.

## Math

Inline math: $x^2 + y^2 = z^2$.

$$
\sum_{n=1}^{4} n = 10
$$

Math remains readable notation, not typeset graphics.

## Images
