## The 120-column option also applies to this deliberately long heading title so that it stays readable on very wide terminals while its decorative rules still fill the available viewport width

On a terminal wider than 120 columns, toggle the 120-column wrapping option in
the command palette. Heading titles wrap at the cap; continuation lines remain
aligned and decoration-free. Switch it off to restore normal viewport wrapping.
